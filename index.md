## 🎯 Key Features

Wisp comes packed with features that any racing fan will appreciate. Here's what you can expect:

### Speedometer with Wheel-Indicated Speed
Wisp shows your exact speed directly on screen. Unlike the in-game speedometer, Wisp uses wheel rotation data to give you an accurate reading even when your tires are slipping or spinning.

### Real-Time Boost Gauge
Monitor your turbo or supercharger boost pressure with a clean, easy-to-read gauge. This helps you stay in the power band and get the best acceleration out of your car.

### Tire Temperature Display
See the temperature of each tire individually. This information is crucial for understanding grip levels, managing tire wear, and knowing when to push harder or back off.

### G-Force Telemetry
The G-force display shows you the forces acting on your car during acceleration, braking, and cornering. This allows you to feel and see exactly how your car is performing dynamically.

### Fully Customizable Appearance
Wisp lets you personalize the HUD to match your preferences. Change colors, sizes, positions, and even which elements are visible. Make it as subtle or as flashy as you want.

### Native Windows Design
Built with WPF (Windows Presentation Foundation), Wisp feels like a natural part of Windows. It's fast, responsive, and won't slow down your gaming experience.
